## What Coverage You Get

A complete spray foam contractor insurance program typically includes:

**General Liability** — The foundation. Covers bodily injury and property damage claims arising from your operations. A proper spray foam GL policy includes off-ratio coverage provisions, modified faulty workmanship language, and pollution carve-outs for SPF chemical exposures. Per-occurrence limits of $1 million to $2 million with matching aggregate limits are standard for most operations.

**Off-Ratio Coverage** — Either as a GL endorsement or standalone, this is the coverage that responds when foam goes wrong because of a mix-ratio error. It's the most important piece of coverage for spray foam contractors and the one most often missing from policies placed by generalist agents.

**Contractor Pollution Liability** — Covers chemical exposure claims, including isocyanate exposure events, odor-related claims, and contamination of adjacent spaces. This can be bundled with GL or placed as a standalone policy depending on your limits and risk profile.

**Workers' Compensation** — With the correct class codes for your specific operations. The right code matters both for coverage (wrong code = potential claim denial) and for price (wrong code = potentially significant overpayment).

**Commercial Auto** — Your rig, your truck, your trailer. Including the proportioner, hoses, and equipment mounted on the vehicle. Physical damage coverage for the equipment itself, liability for accidents on public roads.
